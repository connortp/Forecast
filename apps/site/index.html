How it works

Set it up once. Check it every morning.

  1. STEP 1

    Connect your accounts

    Link checking and credit cards through Plaid. Forecast can read balances and transactions. It can't move money.

  2. STEP 2

    It learns your rhythm

    Forecast finds your paydays, rent, subscriptions and the usual grocery run in your recent history. You confirm what it found.

  3. STEP 3

    Fill your buckets

    Name what you're saving for: an emergency fund, a trip, holiday gifts. Money in a bucket never counts as spendable.

  4. STEP 4

    See one number

    Spend less today and tomorrow's number goes up. Spend more and it adjusts. Nothing to categorize, nothing to reconcile.

Your data

Built to look, never to touch.

Read-only

Accounts connect through Plaid, which banks already use. Forecast sees balances and transactions and can't move a cent.

Not for sale

Your transactions are used to make your forecast. They aren't sold, and they aren't used to show you ads.

Gone when you go

Disconnect a bank or delete your account from settings, and your data is deleted with it.
