Access

Read-only

Accounts connect through Plaid, which banks already use. Forecast sees balances and transactions and can't move money.

Privacy

Not for sale

Your transactions are used to make your forecast. They aren't sold, and they aren't used to show you ads.

Disconnect a bank or delete your account from settings, and your data is deleted with it.
